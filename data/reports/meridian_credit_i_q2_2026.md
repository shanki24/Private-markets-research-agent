# Meridian Private Credit Fund I, Quarterly Report Q2 2026

## Fund overview
Meridian Private Credit Fund I is a 2020 vintage senior direct lending fund with commitments of $1.6 billion. As of June 30, 2026, the fund had called $1.38 billion, or 86.3% of commitments, and the fund uses a subscription facility with a $200 million limit.

## Performance summary
As of June 30, 2026, the fund delivered a net IRR of 10.6%. Net TVPI was 1.31x, DPI was 0.74x and RVPI was 0.57x. Net asset value was $787 million. Distributions during the quarter totaled $58 million, consisting primarily of interest income and loan repayments.

## Portfolio and credit quality
The fund holds 41 loans with a weighted average yield of 11.3% and a weighted average loan-to-value of 42%. Approximately 96% of the portfolio is first lien senior secured debt. Two loans, representing 1.8% of fair value, are on non-accrual status. Cumulative realized losses since inception are 0.4% of invested capital.

## Outlook
Spreads on new originations averaged SOFR plus 525 basis points during the quarter, compared with SOFR plus 575 basis points a year ago. The manager notes increased competition from bank lenders in the upper middle market.

## Fees and expenses
The management fee is 1.0% of invested capital. Incentive fee is 10% above a 6% hurdle.
