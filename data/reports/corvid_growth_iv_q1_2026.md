# Corvid Growth Equity Fund IV, LP Letter Q1 2026

## Fund overview
Corvid Growth Equity Fund IV is a 2022 vintage growth equity fund focused on vertical software and fintech, with total commitments of $780 million. As of March 31, 2026, the fund has called $512 million, or 65.6% of commitments.

## Performance summary
As of March 31, 2026, the fund's net IRR was 6.2% and gross IRR was 11.9%. Net TVPI was 1.12x, DPI was 0.04x and RVPI was 1.08x. Net asset value stood at $553 million. The fund made its first meaningful distribution of $21 million following a secondary sale of shares in Ledgerline.

## Portfolio activity
The fund led a $60 million Series D round in Pathwise Payments and invested $35 million in Tallyform, a construction ERP provider. Two portfolio companies, Brightcove Analytics and Quillon, were marked down by a combined $18 million due to slower revenue growth. The portfolio contains 16 companies.

## Valuation and outlook
Portfolio companies are valued primarily on forward revenue multiples. The median revenue multiple applied was 6.4x NTM revenue, compared with 7.1x a year earlier. Weighted average revenue growth across the portfolio was 38% year over year. The manager expects to complete deployment by the end of 2027.

## Fees and expenses
The management fee is 2.0% of committed capital. Carried interest is 20%, rising to 25% for returns above 3.0x net MOIC.
