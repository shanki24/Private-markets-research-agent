# Halden Infrastructure Fund II, Annual Report 2025

## Fund overview
Halden Infrastructure Fund II is a 2021 vintage European core-plus infrastructure fund with commitments of EUR 2.4 billion. As of December 31, 2025, called capital was EUR 1.71 billion, representing 71.3% of commitments. The fund targets energy transition, digital infrastructure and transport assets.

## Performance summary
At December 31, 2025, the fund generated a net IRR of 9.8% since inception. Net TVPI was 1.24x, DPI was 0.18x and RVPI was 1.06x. The fund's net asset value was EUR 1.81 billion. Cash yield distributed to investors during 2025 totaled EUR 96 million, equivalent to a 5.6% distribution yield on invested capital.

## Portfolio activity
In 2025 the fund acquired a 49% stake in Nordlys Fibre, a Norwegian fibre-to-the-home operator, for EUR 310 million. The fund also completed construction of the 420 MW Valdera solar portfolio in Spain, which reached commercial operation in October 2025. The portfolio comprises 9 platform investments.

## Risk and ESG
Approximately 64% of portfolio revenues are contracted or regulated. The fund reported Scope 1 and 2 emissions intensity of 38 tCO2e per EUR million of revenue, a 22% reduction year on year. Interest rate exposure is 78% hedged through 2028.

## Fees and expenses
The management fee is 1.25% of committed capital during the investment period. Carried interest is 15% above a 7% hurdle.
