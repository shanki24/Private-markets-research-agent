# Northvale Capital Partners Fund III, Quarterly Report Q2 2026

## Fund overview
Northvale Capital Partners Fund III is a 2019 vintage North American mid-market buyout fund with total commitments of $1.25 billion. As of June 30, 2026, the fund has called $1.02 billion, or 81.6% of commitments. The investment period ended in March 2025 and the fund is now in its harvesting phase.

## Performance summary
As of June 30, 2026, the fund reported a net IRR of 17.4% and a gross IRR of 23.1%. Net TVPI stood at 1.86x, with DPI of 0.92x and RVPI of 0.94x. Net asset value was $958 million. Cumulative distributions to limited partners reached $938 million, of which $214 million was distributed during the quarter.

## Portfolio activity
During the quarter the fund completed the sale of Ridgeline Logistics to a strategic buyer, generating gross proceeds of $286 million and a gross MOIC of 3.1x. The fund also made a $45 million add-on investment in Carta Health Systems to support its acquisition of a regional billing platform. The portfolio now consists of 11 active companies.

## Valuation and outlook
Portfolio companies are valued using a blend of comparable trading multiples and discounted cash flow analysis. The weighted average EV/EBITDA multiple applied across the portfolio was 11.2x, down from 11.8x at year-end 2025, reflecting lower public market comparables in industrial services. Net leverage across the portfolio averaged 4.3x EBITDA. Management expects two further exits in the next twelve months.

## Fees and expenses
The management fee stepped down from 2.0% of commitments to 1.5% of net invested capital after the investment period. Carried interest is 20% above an 8% preferred return with a full catch-up.
