# Ashgrove Secondaries Fund V, Quarterly Report Q2 2026

## Fund overview
Ashgrove Secondaries Fund V is a 2023 vintage secondaries fund with total commitments of $3.1 billion. As of June 30, 2026, called capital was $1.94 billion, representing 62.6% of commitments. The fund acquires LP interests and leads GP-led continuation vehicle transactions.

## Performance summary
As of June 30, 2026, the fund reported a net IRR of 21.7%. Net TVPI was 1.38x, DPI was 0.29x and RVPI was 1.09x. Net asset value was $2.11 billion. The fund distributed $163 million during the quarter.

## Portfolio activity
During the quarter, the fund acquired a $420 million portfolio of 14 buyout fund interests at an average discount of 11% to NAV. The fund also led a $275 million single-asset continuation vehicle for Ostrava Packaging. Underlying exposure spans more than 610 portfolio companies across 58 funds.

## Valuation and outlook
Average purchase discounts across the fund are 13% to reported NAV. Approximately 71% of underlying NAV is in buyout strategies, 18% in growth equity and 11% in venture. The manager expects deal volume to remain elevated as LPs seek liquidity.

## Fees and expenses
The management fee is 1.0% of commitments during the investment period. Carried interest is 12.5% above an 8% preferred return.
